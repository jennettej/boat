Persona: Dana, Tour Director

~Dana Larpsson~
Age: 43
Occupation: Boat Tour Director

Background:
Dana is a 43-year-old tour director for Boat Inc. She is very committed to providing the best possible experience
for the customers that she guides on her tours. 
Goals
* Lead succesful tours
* Monitor ticket sales
Frustrations
* Difficulties with monitoring ticket sales on other software
* Convoluted methods to cancel tours that don't have enough ticket sales

Scenario:

Dana is the tour director for a boat tour company. Each tour needs at least half of its seats sold to cover costs, 
so she has to decide ahead of time whether to run it or cancel it. The night before a scheduled sunset cruise, 
she opens the company’s tour management system and selects that tour from the list of upcoming departures. 
The system shows how many tickets have been sold, how many seats the boat holds, and the percentage sold. Dana sees 
that 42 of the boat’s 100 seats are sold, which is 42%. That is below the 50% minimum, so the tour is flagged as at 
risk of cancellation. Dana confirms the cancellation, and the system marks the tour as canceled. It then notifies the
customers who bought tickets and starts their refunds or offers them another date. If the tour had been at 50% or 
higher, the system would show it as confirmed and Dana would take no action.

Stories:

As a tour director, I need to see the percentage of tickets sold for each upcoming tour.

As a tour director, I need to be able to cancel an upcoming tour.

As a tour director, I need the system to notify customers who purchased tickets that the tour is canceled.
